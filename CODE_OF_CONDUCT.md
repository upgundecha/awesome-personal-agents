# Code of Conduct

We welcome contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels. Be respectful, constructive, and patient. Discuss evidence and ideas without attacking people. Respect privacy and give credit for others' work.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, doxxing, spam, and repeated disruption are unacceptable. These expectations apply to all project spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report abusive GitHub content through GitHub's **Report content** option. For other concerns, contact a maintainer through a contact method they make available on their GitHub profile. Do not post private details in public issues. If a concern involves a maintainer, use GitHub's reporting mechanism.

Maintainers will review reports fairly and may remove content, issue warnings, restrict participation, or block contributors according to severity and recurrence. They will protect reporters' privacy to the extent possible and reconsider decisions when new evidence is supplied.
